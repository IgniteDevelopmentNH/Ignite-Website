Everything you might want to know about how we work, what we build, and what it costs. Still have a question? We are one conversation away.

What does Ignite Development build?

Ignite builds custom operational software designed around how your business works. Examples include internal platforms, client portals, dashboards, CRM systems, and AI-powered business tools — all built specifically for your workflows rather than forcing your team into generic software.

Why would a business need custom software?

Most businesses rely on generic tools that were not designed specifically for their workflows. Custom software allows businesses to create systems that match their processes and improve efficiency — instead of adapting how you work to fit the limitations of off-the-shelf products.

Isn't custom software expensive?

Historically, custom software required large development teams and significant budgets. Modern development tools allow Ignite to build powerful systems far more efficiently, making custom software accessible to growing businesses that previously could not consider it.

Do you replace existing software?

Not always. Sometimes the best solution is improving existing processes. Other times, a custom system is the right approach. Ignite recommends the solution that best fits the business — we are not here to sell you software you do not need.

Do you use AI in your software?

Yes, when it creates meaningful value. AI can be integrated into systems to improve workflows, organize information, and help employees work more efficiently. AI is a tool we use to improve operations — it is never the product itself.

Who does Ignite work with?

Ignite primarily works with growing professional service businesses that want to improve their operations through technology — firms that have outgrown spreadsheets and disconnected tools and are ready for systems built around how they actually operate.

Where is software hosted?

Ignite manages deployment using modern cloud infrastructure designed for reliability, scalability, and security. We handle setup and hosting so your system is dependable and ready to grow with your business.
