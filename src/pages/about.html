About Ignite

Custom software for growing businesses.

Ignite Development was founded by Josh Berry and Will Bertoncini, two University of New Hampshire students.

Why we started

We saw small businesses paying substantial amounts for software that didn't fully meet their needs.

We started Ignite to make custom software more accessible to small and growing businesses. We understand both how businesses run and how to build the software behind them, so we work closely with each client to learn their operations and develop around their needs.
